Thanksgiving week is here. Between family traditions, downtown festivities, and the first performances of a holiday classic, Houston offers plenty of ways to celebrate together.

Brittany’s Pick77th Annual H-E-B Thanksgiving Day Parade
Thursday, November 26 · 9 AM | Downtown
Houston’s much-loved parade returns with floats, performers, and a festive morning atmosphere. Check the city’s route and street closure information before heading out.
Begins Friday, November 27Houston Ballet: The Nutcracker
A beloved seasonal tradition returns to the stage with sparkling costumes, memorable music, and holiday magic.
Select evenings, including Thanksgiving nightZoo Lights
An enchanting option for an after-dinner family outing. Note that the zoo is closed for daytime admission on Thanksgiving.
Seasonal nights at Moody GardensHoliday in the Gardens
Plan a Galveston outing centered on lights and seasonal attractions.
Holiday-season weekendsHome for the Holidays in Old Town Spring
Visit the decorated streets and locally owned shops for a slower-paced holiday outing.

Local tip. Downtown parade morning is much easier when you arrive before street closures and choose your parking garage ahead of time. Bring layers—the forecast can change quickly.
